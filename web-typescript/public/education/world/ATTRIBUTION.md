# World map models — CC0

Each `.glb` here packs one asset pack's models as named nodes, scaled to the map's units
(1 unit ≈ 4 m) with textures shrunk to WebP (`web-typescript/tools/build/world-pack.mjs`). Geometry and
colours are unchanged. All packs are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); credit is not required and is given here with thanks.
